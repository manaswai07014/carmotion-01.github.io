## The Story

Nissan and Infiniti are recalling 168,149 Armada, QX56, and QX80 SUVs with 22-inch wheels over incorrect axle weight ratings. Here's what's happening:

The recall concerns an incorrect Gross Axle Weight Rating on the certification label.

In other words, in case you weren’t aware of it or otherwise hadn’t really looked at it all that closely, the label affixed on the driver-side door jamb is full of useful information. You can see your vehicle’s optimum tire pressure, VIN, manufacture date, and even weight ratings. This last item is at the center of the latest recall…

That is — nissan North America is recalling a total of 168,149 vehicles—2020–2026 Nissan Armadas, 2011–2013 Infiniti QX56s, and 2014–2027 Infiniti QX80s—equipped with 22-inch wheels because the Gross Axle Weight Rating (GAWR) on their certification labels is incorrect. Breaking this down, after an internal investigation, Nissan found that its GAWR amount was calculated based solely on the vehicle’s maximum tire pressure rather than the maximum tire pressure and the OEM-recommended pressure identified on the label.
