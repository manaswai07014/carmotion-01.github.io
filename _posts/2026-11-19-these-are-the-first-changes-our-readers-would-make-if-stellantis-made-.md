Talk to anyone who works for an automaker, and they'll tell you journalists make the worst product planners. It's true that no one should hire anyone who's ever done this job to decide which cars get made and which customers' desires to prioritize. Personally, though, I disagree. Commenters are the worst product planners. Except for all of you. Everyone in the Jalopnik comments section is brilliant, attractive, and knows exactly what each OEM needs to do to save their company.

Just for fun, on Monday, we decided to ask all of you what you'd do if Stellantis made you its CEO. Personally, I said I'd quit because no amount of money would be worth the headaches, but not everyone agreed. Plenty of you decided that instead of leaving for happier, more fulfilling pursuits, you'd try to fix things. It's a big job, but somebody's got to do it. Let's take a look at some of the most popular answers. What do you think?. Will any of it work?.

Alexandre Prevot/Shutterstock
Reliability is the #1 priority. Buy some 90's Lexus/Toyota and Acura/Honda and ask "why is this 30+ year old car with 100/200/300+ thousand miles more reliable and better built then the vehicles we have coming off the assembly line today?" Follow that up with more fuel-efficient vehicles for the next gen. Fuel isn't here forever, don't be caught with your pants down, stop ignoring it. Lean on the Pacifica, put in the effort and make this a real contender against Toyota and Honda. Steal those sales. You are the creator of the mini van, take back the crown.

In America, good bye Chrysler. It's been a long run, but the ONLY Chrysler minivans I see on a regular basis have FLEET license plates and bar codes on windows. Merge Ram Truck back into Dodge. It's amazing how many people still call these trucks as Dodge Rams. Correct the mistake and save the money trying to keep an unneeded brand alive. With Dodge, keep the Hemi going for one more generation, but start working on hybrid versions right now as that turbo-6 is going to be the standard engine very soon.

And from what I saw, bring some Peugeots over to the US as Dodges. Some are very attractive. Jonathan Weiss/Shutterstock
Go spy on Toyota and figure out how to make a vehicle with decent build quality.

Chrysler
I always had a vision for Chrysler. I think they need to serve as Stellantis's Cadillac:. Bring back the Chrysler 300 using the Charger EV, start it at $50K, less focus on power, more comfort and agility.

If you want people to take on EVs, you need to make them accessible as possible. As for styling, I'm thinking Italian Mob Boss, some Kings of New York type shiiiet at least for the 300, the 200 can be a more sleeker design. Also I'm de-Kiafying the Pacifica, and I guess I'll add some mid sized SUV because of course we would need one 🙄.
