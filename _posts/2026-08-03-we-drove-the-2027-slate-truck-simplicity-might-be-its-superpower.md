## The Story

We drive a prototype of the 2027 Slate Truck to see if its $26,400 price, hand-crank windows, and minimalist approach make it the affordable electric pickup America has been waiting for.

The tiny EV pickup with big ambitions is fun and reasonably nimble from behind the wheel. Forget the boring battery specs, the onboard NACS port, the money-saving body wraps in a choice of 100 standard colors. For a first drive of a pre-production 2027 Slate Truck, one question is on every mind: How ’bout those hand-crank windows?.

Since you asked, the black plastic rollers work with silicone smoothness, winding us back to the ’70s like some analog time machine. And aside from requiring extra muscle at the fast-food drive-through, the hand cranks show how Slate lavished bonus attention on critical touch points, such as coated-metal interior door handles. The idea is to quell any sense of unacceptable…

The Slate Truck’s emergence from a hotel garage in Newport, Rhode Island—home of gilded seaside mansions, including one owned by Jay Leno—reinforces the radical charm of this small, simple, unpainted pickup. A rail system can append a personal tablet for people who aspire to a Mercedes Hyperscreen.…
