## The Story

Ford is partnering with Apple to bring a new navigation experience to its next wave of electric vehicles. Here's what's happening:

Ford's next-generation EVs will feature native Apple Maps navigation. Apple Maps will replace Google Maps as the built-in navigation system. The new software is expected to improve EV route planning and charging guidance for iPhone users.

In other words, ford is taking its partnership with Apple beyond the basic CarPlay connection. The automaker announced that its next-generation electric vehicles will have Apple Maps built directly into the vehicle's infotainment system. So long, Google.

That is — on top of native integration, Ford says Apple Maps' detailed road-level data will help power the next evolution of its BlueCruise hands-free driving system. The data will reportedly improve the system's ability to operate from highway on-ramp to off-ramp. Ford is clearly taking a different approach than some of its competitors.
