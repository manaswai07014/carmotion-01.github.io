The 2020 BMW M2 Competition in this video keeps a fully stock exterior and interior, but don’t let that fool you — the S55 under the hood is far from factory.

The M2 Competition is finished in Black Sapphire Metallic, which the reviewer says looks particularly menacing when combined with the Competition model's black trim and carbon-fiber accents. The front features the characteristic BMW kidney grilles, adaptive LED headlights, functional cooling openings and visible intercoolers.

The Competition-specific front fascia distinguishes it from the regular F87 M2. The car also has a center hood bulge, aggressive character lines, M-specific mirrors and a black rear lip spoiler.

At the rear are LED taillights, a subtle black spoiler, parking sensors, a diffuser and quad exhaust outlets. Overall, the reviewer describes the F87's rounded, traditional BMW styling as considerably cleaner and more classic-looking than the newer, more angular G87 M2.

Inside, the reviewer appreciates the combination of analog gauges, physical controls and digital technology. Compared with newer BMWs dominated by screens, the F87's interior feels more traditional and driver-focused.
