# CarMotion Daily — Original Content Plan (L4)

## Why Original Content Matters

Google's 2026 Scaled Content Abuse policy targets sites that publish large
volumes of AI-generated/rewritten content without original value. CarMotion
Daily's daily RSS-rewritten articles fall squarely in the risk zone. The
mitigation: a regular cadence of **original, non-RSS content** that
demonstrates editorial value, human oversight, and topical authority.

**Rule**: ≥1 original piece per week. Target: 2/week by month 2.

---

## Content Categories

### 1. Editorial Roundups (weekly)
**Format**: "This Week in [Theme]" — 5-10 stories synthesized into a narrative
- "This Week in EVs: Three new launches, one recall, and a battery breakthrough"
- "This Week in Supercars: Ferrari's quiet hint, McLaren's loud one"
- "This Week in Spy Shots: What we spotted testing"

**Effort**: Low — curator reads the week's own posts, writes a 300-500 word
synthesis with opinion. Can be generated by LLM with a **different** prompt
(no source article; source = our own week of posts).

### 2. Comparison Deep-Dives (bi-weekly)
**Format**: "[A] vs [B]: Which is the better [category]?"
- "Zeekr 9X vs Range Rover: The new Chinese luxury SUV benchmark"
- "Ferrari Luce vs Porsche Taycan: EV poster cars compared"
- "Audi Q9 vs BMW X7: Big SUV showdown"

**Effort**: Medium — pull specs from wiki/database, build a comparison table
in markdown, write 400-600 words of analysis. CarMotion's car-evolution wiki
already has the data.

### 3. Brand Hub Pages (monthly)
**Format**: "/brand/[brand].html" — single page with latest news + history +
key models + timeline
- Start with Ferrari, Porsche, BMW, Tesla (already have wiki data)
- Link all related posts to the hub page
- Takes SEO authority from scattered posts → concentrated hub

**Effort**: High initially, then self-updating (new posts auto-linked).

### 4. "Things We Learned" Listicles (bi-weekly)
**Format**: "5 things we learned about [topic] this month"
- "5 things we learned about solid-state batteries in July 2026"
- "5 things we learned about Ferrari's EV strategy"

**Effort**: Low-medium — curator synthesizes from month's posts.

## Cadence Target

| Day | Content | Type |
|-----|---------|------|
| Monday | Daily 5 posts (auto) | RSS rewrite |
| Tuesday | Daily 5 posts + **Weekly roundup** | RSS + Original |
| Wednesday | Daily 5 posts (auto) | RSS rewrite |
| Thursday | Daily 5 posts (auto) | RSS rewrite |
| Friday | Daily 5 posts + **Comparison deep-dive** | RSS + Original |
| Saturday | Daily 5 posts (auto) | RSS rewrite |
| Sunday | Rest (or weekly roundup alt week) | — |

**Original ratio**: 2/7 days = ~28% original, 72% curated. This is above
Google's quality threshold.

---

## Implementation Path

### Phase 1: Weekly roundup (Week 1)
- Add a `--weekly-roundup` mode to `news_to_website.py`
- Reads past 7 days of `_posts/`, synthesizes into one article
- Prompt: "You are writing a weekly roundup for a car news site. Here are
  the headlines from this week. Write a 300-500 word editorial synthesis."
- Cron: every Tuesday 09:00 HKT (after daily posts)

### Phase 2: Comparison articles (Week 2-3)
- Create `scripts/generate_comparison.py`
- Pulls specs from `wiki/generations/` markdown files
- Generates comparison table + analysis
- Output as Jekyll post with `layout: news-item` and tag `Original`

### Phase 3: Brand hub pages (Week 3-4)
- Create `website/brand/[brand].md` with `layout: default`
- Content: brief history + key models + latest 5 posts (auto-linked)
- Add to index page navigation

### Phase 4: Listicles (Month 2)
- Low priority, can use LLM to synthesize from post history

## Front Matter for Original Content

```yaml
---
layout: news-item
title: "This Week in EVs: Three launches, one recall"
description: "Our weekly roundup of the most important EV stories."
date: 2026-08-05 09:00 +0800
source: CarMotion Daily
source_url: 
original: true
tags: [Electric, Original]
---
```

The `original: true` field lets us filter original content separately and
display a badge ("Original") on the article.

## Risk Notes

- Do NOT let the LLM generate original content without a factual base. For
  roundups, feed it the week's actual post titles + key facts. For
  comparisons, feed it wiki spec data.
- Always label original content with `original: true` in front matter.
- Never publish more than 2 original pieces/day — keep total daily volume
  reasonable (5 curated + max 2 original = 7 max).
