## Daily News Roundup Short

Concept: A 30-60 second Short summarizing the 5 articles published that day.

Script template:
```
[0-5s]  "Here's what happened in cars today."
[5-15s] Story 1 headline + 1-sentence summary
[15-25s] Story 2 headline + 1-sentence summary  
[25-35s] Story 3 headline + 1-sentence summary
[35-45s] Story 4 headline + 1-sentence summary
[45-55s] Story 5 headline + 1-sentence summary
[55-60s] "Read the full coverage — link in description."
```

Description for roundup Short:
```
5 car stories you need to know today — from EV launches to spy shots to classic auctions.

📰 Read all 5 stories: https://carmotion-daily.pages.dev

▸ YouTube: @CarMotion-01
▸ Website: https://carmotion-daily.pages.dev

#carnews #automotive #dailynews #carmotiondaily
```
