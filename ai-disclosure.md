  <h1>AI & Automation Disclosure</h1>
  <p><em>Last updated: August 2026</em></p>

  <h2>How CarMotion Daily Uses AI</h2>
  <p>
    CarMotion Daily uses automated systems to curate and rewrite automotive news
    from publicly available sources, including MotorTrend, Top Gear, evo,
    InsideEVs, Road & Track, Autocar, Jalopnik, Motor1, Autoblog,
    CarAndDriver, and SupercarBlog.
  </p>

  <p>
    Each article is processed using a large language model (LLM) to rephrase
    and summarize the source content. The LLM does not invent facts, numbers,
    or quotes — it restates what the original article reported in fresh wording.
  </p>

  <p>
    We do not claim original reporting credit. Every article links back to its
    original source, and all trademarks and copyrights belong to their
    respective owners.
  </p>

  <h2>What AI Does Not Do</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>No fabrication:</strong> The LLM is instructed to never invent facts, numbers, dates, or quotes that are not in the source article.</li>
    <li><strong>No original journalism:</strong> We do not conduct interviews, attend press events, or break news. We curate and rewrite existing reporting.</li>
    <li><strong>No misleading headlines:</strong> Titles are cleaned and reformulated to accurately reflect the story, not to maximize clicks.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Human Oversight</h2>
  <p>
    While the daily pipeline runs automatically, our editorial team reviews
    output quality, monitors for errors, and can intervene to correct or
    remove articles. Reader reports of inaccuracies can be submitted via
    our <a href="/contact.html">contact page</a>.
  </p>

  <h2>Image Attribution</h2>
  <p>
    Images are extracted directly from original news articles via Open Graph
    protocol or sourced from Wikimedia Commons. Each image includes attribution
    to its source. For takedown requests, please contact us.
  </p>

  <h2>Compliance</h2>
  <p>
    This disclosure is provided in accordance with Google's 2026 AI content
    guidelines and Scaled Content Abuse policy. CarMotion Daily publishes a
    limited volume of articles (5 per day), maintains editorial oversight,
    and provides clear attribution and source links for every article.
  </p>
