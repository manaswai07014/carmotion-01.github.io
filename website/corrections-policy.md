  <h1>Corrections Policy</h1>
  <p><em>Last updated: August 2026</em></p>

  <h2>Our Commitment</h2>
  <p>
    CarMotion Daily is committed to accuracy. While our articles are
    auto-generated from original sources, we recognize that errors can
    occur — in the source material, in the rewriting process, or in
    metadata. We correct them promptly and transparently.
  </p>

  <h2>How to Report an Error</h2>
  <p>
    If you spot a factual error, misattributed quote, incorrect number,
    or any other inaccuracy in an article, please report it via our
    <a href="/contact.html">contact page</a> with:
  </p>
  <ul>
    <li>The article title or URL</li>
    <li>A description of the error</li>
    <li>The correct information (with a source if possible)</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Correction Process</h2>
  <ol>
    <li><strong>Review:</strong> Our editorial team reviews the report within 24 hours.</li>
    <li><strong>Verify:</strong> We cross-check the claim against the original source article.</li>
    <li><strong>Correct:</strong> If confirmed, the article is updated immediately.</li>
    <li><strong>Note:</strong> A correction note is appended to the bottom of the article:</li>
  </ol>

  <blockquote style="background: var(--bg); border-left: 4px solid var(--accent); padding: 16px 20px; margin: 20px 0;">
    <em>Correction (Date): An earlier version of this article [description of error]. This has been updated to [correct information].</em>
  </blockquote>

  <h2>Retractions</h2>
  <p>
    If an article is found to be substantially inaccurate or misleading,
    it may be retracted entirely. Retracted articles remain online with a
    prominent retraction notice, and the URL is preserved to avoid broken
    links.
  </p>

  <h2>Takedown Requests</h2>
  <p>
    Original source publishers who believe their content has been used in
    a way that exceeds Fair Use may request takedown via our contact page.
    We will review and respond within 48 hours.
  </p>
