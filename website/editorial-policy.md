  <h1>Editorial Policy</h1>
  <p><em>Last updated: August 2026</em></p>

  <h2>Mission</h2>
  <p>
    CarMotion Daily provides a clean, ad-free daily snapshot of the most
    interesting automotive news from major publications. Our goal is to make
    it easy for car enthusiasts to stay informed without navigating paywalls,
    popups, or clickbait.
  </p>

  <h2>Content Selection</h2>
  <p>
    Articles are selected from 11 major automotive news RSS feeds. The
    automated pipeline picks the most relevant and interesting stories each
    day, with a maximum of 5 articles published per day to maintain quality
    over quantity.
  </p>

  <h2>Rewriting Standards</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>No verbatim copying:</strong> Every sentence is restated in fresh wording by our LLM pipeline.</li>
    <li><strong>Factual fidelity:</strong> All numbers (horsepower, price, year, range), proper nouns (brand, model, person names), and dates are preserved exactly as in the source.</li>
    <li><strong>Original analysis:</strong> Each article includes a "Why It Matters" section and a "CarMotion Daily's Take" section that provide editorial context beyond the source article.</li>
    <li><strong>Source linking:</strong> Every article includes a prominent link back to the original source for full coverage.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Editorial Values</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Transparency:</strong> We disclose our use of AI and automation. See our <a href="/ai-disclosure.html">AI Disclosure</a>.</li>
    <li><strong>Accuracy:</strong> Errors are corrected promptly. See our <a href="/corrections-policy.html">Corrections Policy</a>.</li>
    <li><strong>Fair use:</strong> We publish short summaries (under 250 words) with links to original sources, under Fair Use principles.</li>
    <li><strong>No clickbait:</strong> Titles accurately reflect the story content.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Content Volume</h2>
  <p>
    To comply with search engine quality guidelines and avoid "scaled content
    abuse" penalties, CarMotion Daily publishes a maximum of 5 articles per
    day. This is a deliberate editorial choice, not a technical limitation.
  </p>

  <h2>Original Content</h2>
  <p>
    In addition to curated news, CarMotion Daily publishes original analysis
    pieces weekly, including comparison articles, brand deep-dives, and
    editorial roundups. These are clearly marked as original content.
  </p>
