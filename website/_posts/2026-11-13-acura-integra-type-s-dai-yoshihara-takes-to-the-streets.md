Can the 2024 Acura Integra Type S live up to one of the most iconic names in JDM performance history?. I handed the keys to Formula Drift champion Dai Yoshihara to find out. Known for his championship-winning drift career, professional racing experience, and decades of car control at the highest level, Dai Yoshihara knows exactly what makes a driver's car special.

In this episode, Dai takes my 2024 Acura Integra Type S to the streets and delivers his honest impressions of Acura's latest performance flagship. Powered by a turbocharged 2.0-liter VTEC engine producing 320 horsepower and 310 lb-ft of torque, the Integra Type S shares its heart with the legendary Honda Civic Type R, but adds its own premium Acura character.

Throughout the drive, we explore how the Integra Type S honors the legacy of the legendary Acura Integra Type R, while carving out its own identity in today's performance car market. Dai breaks down the steering feel, chassis balance, power delivery, exhaust note, and real-world drivability to determine whether Acura has created one of the best sport compact cars on sale today.

The Integra Type S represents Acura performance, blending everyday usability with genuine enthusiast credentials. So, If you're a fan of JDM cars, Acura Integra Type S, and manual transmission sports cars, this is a review you won't want to miss!.

Drive Culture is detailed car review YouTube channel hosted by the ultimate car enthusiast, Jonathon Rivers. Who is Jonathon Rivers?. Well, he's a 15-year-plus auto industry veteran, who was born and raised in the Motor City!. He lived several years in Japan, speaks and reads Japanese, and now works for American Honda Motor. (Although this channel is his own & not affiliated) He's owned several fun cars including an Evo IV, multiple Civic Si, Focus RS, and most recently the Integra Type S (which he led planning for Acura)!.

The central theme is that the Integra Type S offers much of the performance associated with the Honda Civic Type R but delivers it in a more refined, comfortable package that makes it particularly appealing as a daily driver.

The episode features professional driver Dai Yoshihara, originally from Japan, who has lived in Southern California for more than 18 years. Yoshihara is a former professional drift driver who continues to compete in motorsports and regularly works with Honda, Acura and Honda Racing Corporation (HRC).

Because of his experience driving everything from competition vehicles to high-performance street cars, Rivers invites him to evaluate the Integra Type S and take it for a drive.


## Why It Matters

Yoshihara says he would choose the Integra Type S for street driving because the Civic Type R's suspension can feel overly aggressive for everyday use, particularly in its most performance-oriented setting.
