## The Story

The world’s largest retailer is building its own EV fast-charging network to lure EV-driving shoppers.

The world’s largest retailer is building its own EV fast-charging network to lure EV-driving shoppers. Walmart is getting into the EV charging business, and it’s betting big. The world’s largest retailer is installing new DC fast chargers at its stores across America at a rate second only to Tesla. The company already operates gas stations at many of its stores.

Why Is Walmart Building EV Chargers?Interestingly, the company is doing this work rather quietly. When asked by Bloomberg News why it was getting into the low-margin EV charging business, Walmart sent a statement saying simply “our broad footprint positions us to make EV charging more accessible for communities while expanding the way we serve customers.”.
