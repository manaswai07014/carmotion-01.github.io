On Monday, a former Volkswagen engineer confessed to securities fraud in Manhattan federal court. While this is the highest profile incident of insider trading currently taking place within the automotive sector, the industry is perpetually saturated by financial shenanigans.

From May to June 2024, Stamp apparently used numerous brokerage accounts to buy up roughly 9,000 shares and even more stock options. Plank seems to have made far fewer purchases. However, the indictment suggests that a member of his family appears to have done the same. It’s estimated that Stamp had made about $250,000 by trading prior to the official announcement, whereas Plank accrued significantly less.

Despite the two men having said they were not guilty over the summer, Stamp has since changed his tune. “I knew that what I was doing was wrong and a breach of my duty to my employer,” Stamp said at a recent hearing.

Apparently, the smoking gun was the fact that the two men had conducted internet searches pertaining to insider trading shortly before buying shares. Plank had been investigating how the crime was prosecuted while Stamp looked into the statute of limitations. In all cases, these events took place just days before Volkswagen and Rivian formally announced their plans on June 25, 2024.

The play is often for a few select individuals to be made an example of while other parties go unpunished, either due to a lack of evidence or plea deals. This has been seen in instances of criminal regulatory violations, union bribery, and insider trading.

While this doesn’t really excuse the former Volkswagen employees from criminal wrongdoing, it’s worth pointing out that insider trading seems to be rampant within the industry. We’ve seen countless instances of high-ranking executives at EV startups dumping shares ahead of bankruptcy. These are also often the same businesses that leverage “special purpose acquisition companies” (SPACs) as shell companies to quickly spin up IPOs on major stock exchanges and raise an immense amount of capital over a short period of time.
