## The Story
This week's news split along a familiar fault line: automakers are pouring real money into electrified volume models while regulators and buyers quietly reshape what those cars may be called and what they cost. Honda committed capital to hybrids, Polestar signaled a direct shot at the Tesla Model 3, and Mercedes-Benz kept expanding its EV lineup with the CLA350. Meanwhile, Finland's decision to permit Tesla's driver-assist software under a different name proved that branding is now a regulatory battleground, not just a marketing one. In the background, the classic market's runaway prices kept reminding enthusiasts that nostalgia is no longer affordable.
