## The Story

The headline from *TopGear* — The very first Ferrari Luce is being auctioned for charity - Top Gear — sets up a notable development in the car world. Here's the breakdown:

What you are looking at here is the very first Ferrari Luce production car. As if you’d mistake it for anything else.

In other words, and in exciting news for the three people on the Internet that liked Ferrari’s first crack at an EV, this very car is heading up for auction at RM Sotheby’s Monterey sale during the upcoming Car Week. No official estimate on how much it’ll go for, but we are told that it has a ‘value in excess of £800,000’.

That is — that’s largely because, while the standard Luce will cost just under half a million quid, this particular one (Chassis no.0 no less) was customised through the Ferrari Tailor Made programme. Breaking this down, on completion of the bidding, the Luce will be shipped back to Maranello, with the final delivery not actually taking place until the first quarter of 2027. Looks like those that are desperate will have to wait just a little bit longer to get their hands on one….
