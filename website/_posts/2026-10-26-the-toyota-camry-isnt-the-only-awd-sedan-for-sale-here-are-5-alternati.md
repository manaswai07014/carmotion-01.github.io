And despite living in an SUV-saturated world, the Camry shifts numbers. Looking at 2025, its U.S. Sales figures were a whopping 316,185 units. There's also the matter of the Camry being available in all-wheel drive. Yes, for those who want that extra bit of all-weather traction, but don't want to jump on the high-riding SUV brigade, your Camry can send power to all four wheels if you please. In AWD guise, the hybrid-only 2027 Camry gives you 232 horsepower and a sub-7-second 0-60 mph sprint time.

Prices for the 2027 model start at $30,995 MSRP (inclusive of Toyota's $1,295 destination charge) for the LE, rising to $37,395 for the XSE, while the all-wheel drive option adds another $1,525 across all models. That being said, the Camry isn't the only AWD sedan out there. In fact, there's a spate of competitors constantly nipping at the Camry's heels, and sometimes scooting ahead too. To mix things up, we've selected three new options, as well as a couple of used alternatives.

Jonathan Weiss/Shutterstock
Kia's K5 sedan is seeing sales growth, but it only has one model available with AWD: the GT-Line. It starts at $29,935 (including the $1,245 destination fee), with an extra $1,600 required to send power to all four wheels, which undercuts the most affordable AWD Camry on price. The 2027 Kia K5 GT-Line AWD model comes with a 186-horsepower naturally aspirated 2.5-liter four-pot.

If all-wheel drive isn't a must, then you can also consider the K5 GT, which adds a turbo and a sports-oriented dual-clutch automatic transmission. That model makes 290 hp and, although it's front-wheel drive, it will get you a Camry-beating 0-60 mph time of just over five seconds. However, unlike the Camry, the Kia K5 currently has no hybrid option, although rumors suggest that could change.

Unlike the Camry's 8-inch infotainment screen found on lesser trims, the 2027 K5 gives you a 12.3-inch display no matter which trim you select. You can option a 12-speaker Bose sound system that outdoes the Camry's available 9-speaker JBL setup on paper. The K5 is also roomier, with 105 cubic feet of passenger space versus 100 in the Camry, and a 16 cubic-foot trunk that slightly edges what the Camry offers.

Jonathan Weiss/Shutterstock
The Kia K5's platform sibling, Hyundai's Sonata, has the same set of regular gasoline powertrains. And, just like the K5, the all-wheel drive variant is confined to being offered on its mid-tier trim: the SEL Sport. SEL Sport models start from $30,445 (inclusive of Hyundai's $1,295 freight charge), making them slightly dearer than the K5 GT-Line. However, adding AWD to the Sonata SEL Sport costs $100 less than with the K5, at $1,500, and leaves it a touch cheaper than a base 2027 Toyota Camry LE AWD.

The 2026 Sonata offers a 191-horsepower non-hybrid powertrain, which is slightly more powerful than the Kia's 186-hp unit. Hyundai Sonata has one distinct advantage over the K5, though, so long as you're willing to forgo that all-wheel drive capability. The Sonata gets the option of a hybrid drivetrain for the promise of fuel savings, which leaves it with a combined EPA estimate of 51 mpg vs. 27 mpg for the Sonata SEL Sport AWD and Kia K5 GT-Line AWD.

Toyota Camry has a combined EPA estimated mileage of 50 mpg for the LE all-wheel drive variant, but there's another caveat if you're looking for an alternative to it. The Sonata hybrid makes 192 combined horsepower versus the Camry's 232 hp.

Jonathan Weiss/Shutterstock
Toyota and Nissan have been competitors for ages, so it's no surprise that Nissan makes a vehicle that goes head-to-head with Toyota in nearly every segment. There are four 2026 Altima trims available, with prices starting at $30,130 (inclusive of a $1,275 destination charge) for the base SV, but you'll need to add an additional $1,500 to access the all-wheel drive system.

The 2026 Altima is offered with a naturally-aspirated 2.5-liter inline-4 engine putting out 188 horsepower that pairs with a CVT. This powertrain doesn't help the Altima's 0-60 time, which comes in at 7.6 seconds for AWD models. The interior comes standard with a 6-speaker sound system and an 8-inch display for base trims. Buyers can opt for a 9-speaker Bose system and a 12.3-inch display, though. The Altima's safety suite isn't as comprehensive as the Camry; adaptive cruise control, for example, isn't standard across the range.

David S. Swierczek/Shutterstock
Unlike Honda not equipping the long-running Accord with drive to all four wheels, Acura was game for it a couple of years ago. The TLX sedan bowed out in 2025, but there is always the used market. Bear in mind that this was a roughly $46,000 to $58,000 car in 2024, and you can see how premium it is. Today, the TLX has retained value remarkably well, and even used, may command a higher price than a base-spec AWD 2027 Camry.
