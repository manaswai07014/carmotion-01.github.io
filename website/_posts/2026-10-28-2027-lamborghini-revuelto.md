The 2027 Lamborghini Revuelto takes the idea of hybrid motoring and flips it on its head, with a powertrain that combines the brand's signature V-12 engine with three electric motors for a combined 1001 horsepower. Forget fuel economy: With all those ponies galloping at full tilt, this hybrid's acceleration will cause tunnel vision, though the Revuelto can calm down enough for highway cruising as well as low-speed city commutes.

The two-seat cabin is decked out in Lamborghini's finest wares, and the seats are well bolstered for spirited cornering. The interior is a chore to get in and out of, but opening the scissor doors adds drama to the process. The Revuelto is an in-your-face land-bound spaceship, so it's not a good choice for ultra-wealthy introverts; crowds will suddenly appear every time you stop. But if you have the scratch, and you're up for thrilling the throngs at cars and coffees, this is the car you want to drive.

Compare the 2027 Lamborghini Revuelto with the top vehicles in this segment with our new compare tool. The Revuelto gains a 1050-hp SV trim this year. The SV model offers a slight boost in power over the standard Revuelto and wears a number of aerodynamic elements to improve downforce, including a large fixed rear wing.

A limited-edition Revuelto Miura 60° Homage model also joins the lineup, paying tribute to—you guessed it—the legendary Lamborghini Miura sports car from the 1960s. Only 99 will be made, and all of them will feature lower body panels painted in a contrasting color to the rest of the body. The colors offered are all inspired by those offered on the Miura. Unique wheels are also part of the Homage look, as is a special rear Lamborghini script in gloss black. The interior wears leather upholstery on the seats embroidered with the Miura's cannelloni pattern; a carbon-fiber plaque inside indicates that the car is one of only 99 made.

This bull is no ballerina when it comes to performance. The mid-mounted 814-hp 6.5-liter V-12 is naturally aspirated and screams up to a 9500-rpm redline. Of the three electric motors integrated into the powertrain, two drive the front wheels, while the third is integrated into the eight-speed dual-clutch automatic transmission and assists the V-12 gas engine on the rear axle. The total output of this powertrain is 1001 horsepower in regular models and 1050 in the range-topping SV.

From behind the wheel, the Revuelto's acceleration is mind-bendingly intense, with the electric motors providing an instant surge of power off the line and the V-12 howling as you explore the upper reaches of its power band. Handling is sharper and more agile than in the outgoing Aventador, and the ride is remarkably civil if you put the car in Soft mode. The Revuelto benefits from rear-wheel steering and a torque-vectoring system, which gives it more stability in corners and makes it easier to nip apexes. We also drove the SV and found it to be even more capable on the racetrack.

The Revuelto is a plug-in hybrid, but not in the traditional sense of the term. This is no Prius competitor, out to hypermile its way from point A to point B. Its 3.8-kWh battery pack, nestled in the space between the two bucket seats, provides just enough juice to power the Revuelto for about 5 miles on electric power alone.

That's enough to slip away unnoticed from your neighborhood should you need to leave early on a Saturday morning without waking the neighbors, but not enough to avoid using lots of gasoline on just about every drive. The Revuelto is a performance hybrid, using the electricity in its battery—and the three electric motors it powers—to add a bolt of lightning to the powertrain's output.
