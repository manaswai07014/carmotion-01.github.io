It then goes beyond the Toyota by featuring more premium interior materials and wrapping it all in stylish sheet metal with an updated version of Lexus’s trademark spindle grille.

A hybrid powertrain comprising a turbocharged 2.4-liter four-cylinder engine and two electric motors makes a combined 366 horsepower; a six-speed automatic transmission and all-wheel drive are standard.
