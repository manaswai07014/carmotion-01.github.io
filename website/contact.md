  <p>
    Original source publishers who believe their content has been used in a way
    that exceeds Fair Use may request takedown. We review and respond within
    48 hours. Please include:
  </p>
  <ul>
    <li>The specific article URL on CarMotion Daily</li>
    <li>The original source URL</li>
    <li>Your relationship to the original publisher</li>
  </ul>
